# Pro player ratings

Static website (docs/) plus a Python rating pipeline (pipeline/).

## Layout
- pipeline/rating_core.py: the rating formula (role z-scores, early/late scores, shrinkage). The notebooks call it.
- pipeline/team_elo.py: team strength (Elo) from results, through all years in one run (settings at the top: WEIGHT, TIER_PRIOR, LEAGUE_PRIOR, TITLE, GRID_* for K/decay/roster cut, ROSTER_DAYS). Game weights: Worlds > MSI > FST/EWC > WSCI > leagues > events with major teams / EWC qualifiers > EM and other events; league levels fitted on the whole season with a prior per tier (major leagues about 60 apart: LCS, then LCP and CBLOL lower; tier 2 far below, its best team just above the weakest major teams); one Elo change per series (Bo1/Bo3/Bo5); title bonus for winning a league split or event; league levels per league and year; decay towards the league level after 14 idle days (half-life 90 days); each player new to a team (no games for it in 120 days) cuts the team's own level by 10%; boards without a split/stage filter show Elo now and only teams that played in their league's latest split (final stages like VCS Finals don't count as a split). Team rosters: starting lineup and subs (in the latest split, still on the team), former, event stand-ins. K 48 (K, half-life and roster cut chosen 2026-11-01 from a log-loss test on all 2024-26 series; faster decay predicted worse).
- pipeline/build_site_data.py: builds docs/data/ from every <year>_LoL_esports_match_data_from_OraclesElixir.csv in pipeline/ (settings at the top: FILES, REFERENCE_YEAR, YEARS (per-year TIERS, BASELINE_GROUPS, SPLIT_MAP, UNLABELLED, TOUR_TIERS; the plain ones are REFERENCE_YEAR's), BASELINE_GROUPS, TIERS, INTERNATIONAL, EVENTS, QUALIFIERS, LEAGUE_NAMES, K, SPLIT_MAP). lec_rating_site_data.ipynb just runs it.
- pipeline/fetch_data.py + .github/workflows/update-data.yml: daily Action that downloads the CSVs (Drive ids in pipeline/data_sources.json), rebuilds and opens/updates a PR from the data-update branch. index.json "data_until" (latest game date, shown in the footer) replaces a build timestamp, so rebuilding the same data changes nothing.
- docs/data/ is generated, never edit it by hand: index.json (config, player and team index, tournaments and qualification links), summary/<year>.json (sums per player x league x split x stage, used by the leaderboards), games/<year>/<league>.json (games, loaded on player pages), teams/<year>/<league>.json (team-games, loaded on team pages; summary/<year>.json "teams" holds the team leaderboard sums).
- docs/: index.html, app.js, style.css. Loads the data files with fetch. No frameworks, no build step.
- The leaderboard math (rateSums in app.js) and the summary sums (write_site_data in build_site_data.py) must stay in step.

## Rules
- Never commit CSV files. The Oracle's Elixir data stays local (.gitignore covers *.csv).
- These are performance ratings relative to the games counted, not skill. Keep that wording on the site.
- Every league in the file with 30+ complete games is on the site with its own baseline; LCK/LPL/LEC/LCS share one (BASELINE_GROUPS), international games have theirs. Ratings of the main leagues must not change when leagues are added. Each year is rated on its own (own baselines; c(role) calibrated per year and the game ratings put on REFERENCE_YEAR's scale), so adding a year never changes another year's numbers. Leagues with only partial data (LPL 2024-25, MSI 2024) have no player ratings but are on the team side (MIN_TEAM_GAMES).
- Every league has a tier per year (TIERS, YEARS): Major (LCK, LPL, LEC, LCS; 2025: LTA North instead of LCS), Tier 1 (LCP, CBLOL; 2025: LTA South, LCP; 2024: CBLOL, PCS, VCS, LJL, LLA, LCO), Tier 2 (default) or Tier 3, shown on the site. The "Major leagues" board combines the major leagues and international games; "All leagues" combines every game.
- Events (EVENTS: cups like EMEA Masters, KeSPA Cup) and qualifiers of international events (QUALIFIERS: EWC qualifiers; a region's own Worlds qualifier filed under WLDs = the games before the event's last 14+ day break) are rated per team against its home league's baseline, count 1× in the overall rating, and are not international games. Events get their own board.
- Only major leagues use the common Winter/Spring/Summer names (SPLIT_MAP). Other leagues keep their real split names; unlabelled games join the nearest split, else get a name from the schedule.
- A player is name + role, split into separate players only when several playerids never shared a team (in REFERENCE_YEAR); other years join a REFERENCE_YEAR player by playerid + role, else are playerid + role under their latest name. Players from before phase 1 come first in the index so their page addresses stay the same.
- Teams: Elo uses every game (partial ones too) and is a win-based strength, separate from the player performance ratings; adding teams or Elo must never change player numbers. Team key = teamid, else the name (REFERENCE_YEAR's teams first in the index). Series = same league and two teams, games under 12 hours apart (may cross midnight), split where the game number does not go up.
- Year filter: leaderboards and the main page open on the latest year (st.year); player and team pages on the latest year the player or team played (st.pyear, st.pdef), with a career table of every year on player pages. The Major leagues board only lists players and teams with major-league games in the selection.
- Site pages: #home (menu), #players (player leaderboards), #teams (team leaderboards), #events (tournaments), #p-... player pages, #t-... team pages, #e-... tournament pages.
- Tournaments (tournaments() in build_site_data.py): one per year x league x kind x split, from the team-games. League games without a split label in the data (e.g. "TCL Oct", "CD Feb-Mar") are in no tournament, except a major league's unlabelled games listed in UNLABELLED (LCS Oct 5-13 = "LCS Promotion", a Tier 2 tournament via TOUR_TIERS, with no common split name); a league with no split labels at all keeps its schedule-named splits (LFL Split 1-3). Champion = the title rule used for Elo (finals()). Formats and stages: FORMATS in build_site_data.py, hand-written from Riot's announcements and press coverage (per year; stages by the playoffs flag or by dates); tournaments not listed get regular season + playoffs from the data, or one list of results. Standings tables only for "table" stages (round robin, Swiss), marking the teams that reached the next stage; the stage with the final shows places (placings() in the pipeline) and the tournament each team qualified for with its place there. "Qualified from" is grouped by region (the team's home league) and shown below the stages. Qualification links (tournament_links()) come only from teams that played both tournaments, never from a hand-made list.
- Trophy cabinet (awards() in build_site_data.py, index.json "awards"): titles and runner-up finishes (no top 4) for teams and for every player who played for the team in that tournament (marked sub if they missed the final); awards by rating, labelled as ours and not official: Tournament MVP (a top 4 team, at least half of the team's games; picked by rating + MVP_PLACE_BONUS for 1st/2nd/3rd-4th) and Finals MVP (champion, final series) for major-league splits and international events, and Player of the Year (overall and per role) per major league and year. Official awards (OFFICIAL_AWARDS in build_site_data.py: MVPs, Finals MVPs, All-Pro 1st-3rd teams) are hand-written from the leagues' announcements and press coverage, only where sources agree; players are matched by name in that tournament (or league and year, split "*"), shown as official and kept apart from the awards by rating.
- Split fixes per year (YEARS): MERGE_SPLITS (LPL placement stages join their split), DATE_SPLITS (LTA 2025 cross-conference games: Split 1 Playoffs, Championship), TOUR_NAMES (names shown for data split names: LCK Road to MSI / Season Playoffs, LEC Season Finals 2024; "" = the league name alone, e.g. Demacia Cup; also passed to the site as config.split_names).
- Keep docs/ working as plain static files (GitHub Pages serves /docs).
- Do not add the unofficial lolesports feed to the repo.
- Ask before changing the rating formula or weights, and say how rankings would change.

## Commands
- Build data: cd pipeline, then python build_site_data.py (add --report for the top 10 per role and checks per baseline)
- Preview: cd docs, then python -m http.server 8000, then open http://localhost:8000
- Python 3.14; install packages with python -m pip install.
